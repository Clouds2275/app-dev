#**My Favorite series is Gurren Lagann the Movie: The Lights in the Sky Are Stars.** 

***It is the story of Simon and Team Dai-Gurren rising from peace into a cosmic battle to save all life, proving that human spirit can outshine even the stars themselves. In a peaceful future, Simon the Digger—once a shy boy who followed his bold friend Kamina—has grown into a calm, thoughtful leader. Humanity has finally reached the surface and built a thriving civilization. The war against the Beastmen is long over, and Simon and Nia are preparing for their wedding. It seems like the world has finally found its rhythm.***
***But peace does not last.***
***One day, strange energy erupts across the planet. Massive, moon-sized machines called Anti-Spirals awaken, declaring that humanity’s growing ambition is a danger to the universe. They believe that Spiral beings—humans included—will eventually evolve so fast that they could collapse reality itself. To prevent this, they decide to wipe humanity out before it can grow any further.***
***The moon begins to fall toward Earth, revealed to be a weapon meant to trigger humanity’s extinction. Panic spreads. Simon, who once saved the world with sheer determination, is suddenly blamed for everything. He is arrested, and the world’s leaders try to handle the crisis without him.**

***When all hope seems lost, Simon breaks free and reunites with his old comrades. They summon their legendary machine, Gurren Lagann, and stop the moon’s descent—only to discover that the Anti-Spirals have kidnapped Nia and turned her into a messenger of despair. She warns Simon that the Anti-Spirals will never allow humanity to survive.***

***Team Dai-Gurren launches into space, pushing past impossible odds. They fight colossal Anti-Spiral fleets, each battle more surreal than the last galaxies used as weapons, reality bending under pressure. Their machine evolves into Super Galaxy Gurren Lagann, then into the universe-sized Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann, fueled entirely by their will.***
***Inside the Anti-Spiral realm, Simon finally reaches Nia. The Anti-Spiral King tries to crush their spirit, showing visions of hopeless futures. But Simon answers with the belief Kamina taught him long ago:***
**“Believe in the you who believes in yourself.”**
***Their determination shatters the Anti-Spiral dimension.***
***The Anti-Spirals are defeated, and the universe is saved. Nia, however, was created by Anti-Spiral power. With their defeat, her body begins to fade. She and Simon share a quiet, bittersweet wedding, and then she disappears, leaving Simon alone but proud of the world they saved together.***

***Years later, Simon wanders the world as a humble traveler. Humanity thrives among the stars, guided by the legacy of Team Dai-Gurren. Simon smiles, knowing that the future belongs to those who keep moving forward.**
